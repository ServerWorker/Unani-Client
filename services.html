Your visit

What to expect when enquiring

A simple preparation guide for a more useful conversation.

Contact first

Call the clinic to confirm hours, appointment availability, fees and whether your concern can be assessed there.

Bring relevant information

Bring your current medication list, relevant reports, allergies and notes about symptoms or questions.

Clarify the plan

Ask about benefits, risks, alternatives, follow-up and when you should seek additional medical care.

Everyday heart-health basics

For general heart health, discuss practical steps such as balanced eating, regular activity appropriate to your condition, avoiding tobacco, sleep, and checking blood pressure, blood sugar and cholesterol as advised by a qualified clinician.

These general steps do not replace individual medical advice, diagnosis or treatment.

When symptoms may be urgent

Chest pressure or pain, severe breathlessness, fainting, or symptoms that could signal a heart attack need urgent medical assessment. Do not wait for a clinic appointment or rely on a traditional remedy for emergency symptoms.

Seek emergency medical help immediately.
